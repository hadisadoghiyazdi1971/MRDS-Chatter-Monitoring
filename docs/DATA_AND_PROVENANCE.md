# Data and provenance

The study used 160 complete acoustic recordings from 40 machining conditions.
The raw acoustic recordings and MAT archive are not distributed in this
repository. They remain subject to the authors' data-access and sharing
decision.

The frozen condition-disjoint split metadata, dataset manifest, and descriptor
field list are included in `reproducibility/`. Results retain the source run
metadata needed to identify the final policy.

The canonical manuscript PDF is byte-identical to the `Manuscript (1).pdf`
review copy in the final submission bundle used to construct this snapshot.
The package does not contain experimental outputs from earlier MRDS variants,
post-projection refinement studies, numerical sensitivity analyses, or Random
baselines beyond the primary 10% retention setting.
